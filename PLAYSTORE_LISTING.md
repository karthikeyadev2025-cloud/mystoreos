## FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 chars)

```
MyStoreOS is a complete billing and point-of-sale (POS) app built for Indian retail shops, kirana stores, medical stores, and general stores. Run your entire shop from one app — create GST invoices in seconds, track inventory in real time, and see exactly how your business is doing every day.

Whether you run a single counter or manage staff across branches, MyStoreOS gives you fast billing, clear stock control, and simple reports without the cost and complexity of traditional billing software.

WHY SHOPKEEPERS CHOOSE MYSTOREOS

Bill faster at the counter
Generate professional GST invoices in seconds. Scan barcodes, add products, apply discounts, and share the bill on WhatsApp or print it instantly. Accept payments by UPI, cash, or card.

Track your inventory automatically
Every sale updates your stock in real time. Get low-stock alerts so you never run out of fast-moving items. Add products with HSN/SAC codes, categories, SKUs, and variant prices.

Understand your sales
See today's sales, daily day-book, revenue charts, and top-selling products at a glance. Make smarter buying decisions with built-in demand insights.

Manage staff and branches
Add staff with their own logins, set roles, and track who sold what. Run multiple branches and transfer stock between them.

Built for India
Full GST support with HSN/SAC codes and state codes. UPI-first payments. Works on low-cost Android phones. Designed for kirana, medical, garment, electronics, and general retail stores.
```
