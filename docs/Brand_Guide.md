# 🏪 Brand Guide: MyStore OS

Consistent branding is essential for building trust with shopkeepers. 

## 1. Brand Identity
* **Name:** MyStore OS
* **Tagline:** Your Shop. One App. Zero Paper.
* **Tone:** Professional, direct, supportive, and localized.

## 2. Core Color Palette
The application utilizes a dark "glassmorphism" aesthetic to feel premium, modern, and distinct from legacy competitors like Vyapar.

* **Primary Action (Red/Pink):** `#f43f5e` - Used for primary buttons, highlights, and the brand logo icon.
* **Secondary Action (Purple):** `#8b5cf6` - Used in gradients alongside the primary color to create depth.
* **Success (Green):** `#10b981` - Used for "Paid" credits and "Accepted" orders.
* **Warning (Yellow):** `#f59e0b` - Used for "Pending" orders and offline warnings.
* **Background Dark:** `#0f172a` - The deep slate background that allows the glass cards to pop.

## 3. Typography
* **Primary Font:** `Outfit` (Google Fonts)
* **Usage:** `Outfit` provides a clean, geometric, and highly readable look on mobile devices, ensuring accessibility for shopkeepers of all ages.

## 4. Logo & Iconography
* **Primary Icon:** The `Store` icon from Lucide-React.
* **Style:** All iconography should be clean line-art (stroke-width: 2) to maintain the modern software feel.
* **Marketing:** When sharing on WhatsApp, always include the Store Emoji 🏪 as a prefix to the brand name.

## 5. Positioning against Competitors
**Vyapar:** "Vyapar is a back-office tool. MyStore is a front-office AND back-office tool. We bring you customers via WhatsApp."
**Dukaan:** "Dukaan is complex and English-heavy. MyStore is dead simple, fast, and requires zero setup from your end."
