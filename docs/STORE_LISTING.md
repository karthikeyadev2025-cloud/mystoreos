## Full Description

**MyStore OS** is India's smartest billing and inventory management app for kirana stores, pharmacies, electronics shops, and all retail businesses.

**Stop using paper ledgers and WhatsApp for bills — go digital in 2 minutes.**

### 🧾 BILLING & POS
• Create GST and non-GST bills in seconds
• Standard Bill | Estimate/Quote | Delivery Challan
• Share bills via WhatsApp instantly
• UPI QR code on every bill
• Print receipts on any Bluetooth printer

### 📦 INVENTORY MANAGEMENT
• Track unlimited products with stock alerts
• Batch numbers and 90-day expiry tracking
• Barcode scan to add products to bill
• Bulk restock from distributor catalogue

### 🧠 AI DEMAND FORECASTING (PRO+)
• AI predicts which products will run out next week
• Smart reorder suggestions based on your sales history
• Day-of-week seasonality detection
• Never overstock or understock again

### 📊 REPORTS & ANALYTICS
• 30-day profit & loss
• Cash flow tracking
• Top 10 products by revenue
• Tally ERP / GST export (Enterprise)
• GSTR-1 CSV for CA filing

### 💳 CREDIT & CUSTOMERS
• Full customer CRM with purchase history
• Credit book — track who owes you
• Loyalty points system
• WhatsApp payment reminders

### 🔒 SECURE & RELIABLE
• Cloud sync — access from any device
• Works offline (PWA technology)
• Razorpay-secured payments
• Supabase backend with daily backups

### 📱 DESIGNED FOR INDIA
• Telugu & English language support
• UPI-first payments
• Indian date/number formatting
• CGST/SGST/IGST compliant invoices
